# Wood running short, and what comes of it

Written 2026-11-25 after the owner's question: once the trees near the village are gone, what happens?

## What is built now

- Every tree holds a fixed amount of timber. Clearing ground for houses and fields leaves logs, and those are used first.
- Building and the hearths use up the stack. Then people walk out to fell, carrying back what they can.
- A timber trip is a real walk. The further the trees, the more of the day goes on walking, and the want of toil rises (`T.need.toil`). That want already leads to carts and the wheel.
- Firewood is the main use of wood, as it was in history. Hearths burn every cold step.

## What should follow, each from a felt cause

1. **Woodcutters as a trade.** When timber trips are long and building waits on wood, someone takes up felling as their work. The `woodcutter` job exists; it should be taken up where timber is short and paid where coin exists.
2. **The wood camp.** A woodcutter whose walk to the trees takes most of the day builds a shelter by the woods, fells there, and stacks the timber. Their household may follow. This is the first hamlet, and it needs more than one settlement on the map (phase J1).
3. **Carting and the road.** Timber stacked at the camp is carried to the village by carters. The way between them is worn by every trip (`tread`) and becomes a road (`T.road`), which makes the trip quicker. That is the first long road.
4. **Planting trees.** When the nearest stands are far and still getting further year on year, the place feels it. The answer is an idea:
   - **Woodlot or coppice.** A lot near the village is kept for trees. Saplings are planted there (tree growth already exists in the land model), and stools are cut back every few years rather than felled.
   - **Hedgerows.** These are the field fences built today, grown as living hedges. They give sticks for the fire.
   - Like every idea, it works only through what is built and worked: the woodlot lot and someone cutting it.
5. **Later ages.** Charcoal for the smithy and kilns burns wood fast. When wood is short and dear, peat or coal follows where the land has it.

## Why this order

Steps 2 and 3 need more than one place on the map (phase J1 in `docs/PLAN.md`). Step 4 can come first and fits the village as it is. Step 1 is mostly there already.
