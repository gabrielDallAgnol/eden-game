# Humankind: the full technology tree, read from the game wiki

Read on 2026-11-23 from humankind.fandom.com through the wiki's public listing (the page "Technology" and the 92 technology pages). This replaces the fan-ruleset numbers in `humankind_and_civilization.md`, which were unverified. The wiki is edited by players; it is not the game's own data.

| **Total** | **92** | | **689,980** |

- The Neolithic age has no technologies. One removed technology (Horsemanship) is still listed on the wiki page and is not counted.
- A technology can be researched only in the age the empire has reached (Scientist cultures: one age ahead).
- A technology with several prerequisites needs **only one** of them, so technologies can be skipped.
- Each technology researched raises the cost of all unresearched ones by 1%.

## How much of the tree the space race needs

Rocket Science unlocks the Space Station project; Space Station unlocks Lunar Lander (2,500 industry, 2 oil, 1 aluminium), which unlocks Mars Colony (2,500 industry, 3 oil, 2 aluminium, 1 uranium, +300 fame). "Mars conquered" ends the game in the default and Space Race settings; the winner is still whoever has most fame.

| Goal | Every listed prerequisite | With the one-prerequisite rule (cheapest route) |
|---|---|---|
| Rocket Science | 30 of 92 technologies, 17% of all science | 14 technologies, 12% of all science |
| Space Orbital | 32 of 92 technologies, 22% of all science | 15 technologies, 16% of all science |

Cheapest route to Rocket Science: Calendar → Writing → Philosophy → Rhetoric → Theology → Alchemy → Movable Typeface → Steam Engine → Propeller → Combustion Engine → Aeronautics → Aerial Warfare → Radar → Rocket Science.

(The age gate still applies: each age also needs 7 Era Stars, which this count ignores.)
