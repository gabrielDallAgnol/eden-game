# Dawn of Man and The Universim: technology data read from the game wikis

Read on 2026-11-23 from dawnofman.fandom.com and theuniversim.fandom.com through the wikis' public listings. Both wikis are written by players. The Dawn of Man wiki is fairly complete. The Universim wiki is small (44 pages), mostly written during early access, and covers only the Stone Age research list; it does not describe the released game's later ages or the creator powers.

## Dawn of Man

- The wiki says 56 technologies; 55 separate technology pages are filed under the six age categories (Spirituality is filed under two ages).
- Knowledge points pay for technologies. A point is earned the 1st, 5th, 10th, 50th, 100th and 500th time a thing is gathered, hunted, crafted or built; each milestone gives 2. The wiki's own advice shows the exploit: "any building times 5 gives a knowledge point. You can build and then demolish structures to reach this."
- Paleolithic technologies cost 5 points. Each later age opens only after its "main tech" is bought (15 to 20 points): Pottery, Cereal Domestication, Copper Smelting, Bronze Smelting, Iron Smelting.
- Entering an age changes the world without the player choosing it: the wiki says mammoths and woolly rhinos die out when the Copper Age begins.
- Shape (from the in-game tree screenshot `ZeroTechTreeUpdate1.6.00.png`): about a dozen thin lanes run left to right across the six ages, each a chain of one to three technologies per age with almost no links between lanes. The lanes are tools and metal, mining, building, water, archery and walls, armour, crops, animals, cloth, transport, and belief.

## The Universim (Stone Age research list only)

- The wiki lists 65 Stone Age research items with a time each. Each is paid for with time alone: from 10 seconds (Primitive Tools) to 15 minutes; together 378 minutes of research time. 2 items are paid with "Discovery Points" instead.
- Up to five items wait in a queue, and an Auto-Research switch starts the next one without the player.
- A requirement is one or two earlier items, sometimes a building (Town Hall).
- Research items ("perks") come in four colours: blue Evolution perks move the civilisation toward the Space Era and unlock buildings; yellow, green and purple perks are small, rare and very rare bonuses.
- The wiki names four ages: Stone, Medieval, Modern, Space. Its Medieval page says that age begins "with the advent of Electricity". Its Medieval and Modern research tables are empty.
- Shape (from the early-access screenshot `The universim research tree.png`): a web spreading right from one root, Primitive Tools, into short chains for food, water, storage, healing and burial.
