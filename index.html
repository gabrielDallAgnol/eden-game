Year 0

Eden

A family in a valley. Nothing here is scripted: warmth, water and hunger decide everything. Watch for a while: people fish, pick, carry and build, children play, animals graze, rest together, raise their young and hunt.

  • Drag to look around, pinch or scroll to zoom.
  • Your hand is your only power. Press on a thing to lift it, let go to set it down: a person, a beast, a tree, a stone, fire, a handful of water, a house.
  • What follows is the world's doing. A stone set on stone strikes a spark. Fire burns what will burn. Water puts it out.
  • People see what you do. Help them and they come to trust whoever is there; hurt them and they fear it. In time one of them may ask your name.

Days pass slowly and nights fall; a year takes about eight minutes. The button at the top right speeds time up (4x, 16x, 48x: a year in ten seconds) or pauses it.
