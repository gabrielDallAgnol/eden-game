Eden

A family in a valley. Nothing here is scripted: warmth, water and hunger decide everything. Watch for a while: people fish, pick, carry and build, children play, animals graze, rest together, raise their young and hunt.

  • Hand: drag to look around, pinch to zoom, pick up any creature and set it down elsewhere.
  • Rain: hold to water the ground. On frozen ground it falls as snow.
  • Sun: hold to warm. It melts snow in the cold; in a dry heat it parches.
  • Grow: hold and watch plants rise. Weak on frozen or parched ground.
  • Strike: kills, and sets dry things alight.

Days pass slowly and nights fall; a year takes about eight minutes. The button at the top right speeds time up (1x, 3x, 8x) or pauses it.
